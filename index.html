01 — Background

How I think, not just what I know.

Currently: MBA, Business Analytics & Data Science

I don't treat data as a report to file — I treat it as the raw material for a decision someone still has to make.

My background sits at the intersection of business strategy and applied analytics. I've spent the last few years moving between inventory floors and dashboards — automating MIS reporting for a 5,000+ SKU pharmaceutical business, then formalizing that instinct through an MBA in Business Analytics & Data Science and hands-on work applying Generative AI to real datasets.

What ties it together is a habit: before I build a chart or a model, I ask what decision it's supposed to change. If a dashboard doesn't shift someone's next action, it's decoration, not analysis.

DECIDE I start every analysis by naming the decision it needs to inform — not the metric, the decision. Metrics follow.
VALIDATE Numbers get cross-checked before they get trusted. A wrong dashboard is worse than no dashboard.
SIMPLIFY Complex models are easy. Explaining them to a stakeholder in one sentence is the actual skill.
ADAPT I use AI-assisted workflows — prompt engineering, GenAI data prep — as leverage, not a replacement for judgment.
