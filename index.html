My background sits at the intersection of business strategy and applied analytics. What ties it together is a habit: before I build a chart or a model, I ask what decision it's supposed to change. If a dashboard doesn't shift someone's next action, it's decoration, not analysis.

DECIDE Start every analysis by naming the decision it needs to inform — not the metric, the decision. Metrics follow.

GOVERN AI-generated answers get gated — evidence checks, confidence scores, explicit limitations. Never present a guess as a fact.

CASE FILE 03 · SALES & MARKETING AI BUILT

BrightBridge — Sales & Marketing AI Agent

A CRM-style sales intelligence app that tells a sales team who to call, what to say and what is slipping — with every number on screen calculated from CRM data by deterministic code, and AI used only for the language work around it: drafting emails, answering questions, shaping campaigns.

PythonGradioSQLiteGemini APIPlotlyCRM Analytics
What the app covers — ten working tabs
DashboardLeads, active opportunities, pipeline value, won revenue, win rate, weighted pipeline
CRMFilter by industry, stage, source, priority, deal value and date; click a row for full history
Lead PriorityScored leads so reps start with the deals most likely to move
Pipeline & Follow-upsStage view plus due / overdue tracking with a next action per lead
AI Email & MarketingGemini-drafted outreach and campaign content grounded in the lead record
AI Assistant, Import & SettingsAsk questions in plain language, load your own CRM data, configure the agent
Design principle — AI talks, code counts
  • Deterministic numbers. Pipeline value, win rate and weighted pipeline are computed in code from the CRM tables — the dashboard states this on screen, so a figure is never an LLM guess.
  • Action over reporting. The follow-ups view turns 83 open follow-ups into a worklist: 68 overdue, 15 due today, each with a score and a next action.
  • One workspace for sales and marketing. Lead data, outreach email and campaign work sit in the same app, so the message is written with the deal context in front of it.
  • Bring your own data. An Import Data tab loads a company's own CRM records instead of the demo dataset.
